Can a walking robot adapt its gait to the gravity of another world?

The question

Moon gravity is about 1.62 m/s² and Mars gravity about 3.73 m/s², against 9.81 m/s² on Earth. A gait tuned on Earth may not suit those worlds. This project tests a simple idea: slow the walking rhythm down with gravity, using f = f0·√(g / 9.81), and compare it with a gait that keeps the same frequency everywhere.

The short answer

The gravity-scaled frequency delayed the first gait's fall by 13.3% on the Moon and 11.4% on Mars, but did not stop it. A parameter search then made both controllers walk for 12 s without falling. The search mattered far more than the frequency law, and neither controller was best in every respect.

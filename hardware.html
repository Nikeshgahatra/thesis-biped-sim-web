Hardware prototype

A small two-legged prototype was built around an Arduino Mega 2560. It sets the joint layout and the foot length used in the simulation, and it shows what the real servos can and cannot do. All gravity experiments were done in simulation, because gravity cannot be changed in a laboratory.

How it connects to the simulation

  • Same six-joint layout: hip, knee and ankle per leg, all in the pitch plane.
  • Same foot length, 14 cm.
  • Gait amplitudes found in the simulation are small (about 3° to 12°) and the frequencies are below 1 Hz, which suits hobby servos.

Status and limits

  • The joints move through a limited range, but the prototype does not walk on two legs.
  • All joints bend in one plane, so there is no active sideways balance.
  • An MG996R gives about 0.9 to 1.1 N·m at stall, while the simulated motor allows 2.0 N·m. On Earth a modest lever arm can already reach the stall torque.
  • No measurements were taken on the hardware, so none are reported.
